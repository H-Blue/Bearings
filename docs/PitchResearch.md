# Bearings pitch: evidence, claims and ideas to retain

Prepared October 11, 2026. These notes preserve the discussion separately from implementation steps. The pitch should use one need statistic, one grounded story and one concise AI explanation. Evidence informs the design; it does not establish Bearings' efficacy or imply endorsement by Yale.

The expanded [ResearchEvidence.md](ResearchEvidence.md) contains 37 source cards with study designs, findings, limitations, feature implications, pitch claims and a future evaluation path. It includes adult online/workplace training, labeling, journaling, self-distancing, breathing, positive memories, goal review, empathy, human outreach, privacy and contrary evidence. Start with its six-source shortlist.

## Main message

Bearings is designed to help people put precise words to experiences, learn emotional concepts, inspect possible patterns and choose a useful next step. The story is central: vague feeling → specific feeling/context → correct an assumption → clear communication → later review.

Owner's retained line: “AI is changing how we work while understanding ourselves, communicating needs, and collaborating remains valuable.”

Granularity is differentiating emotions in context, not simply generating sophisticated labels. Emotional intelligence is broader than vocabulary. The writer chooses/corrects interpretations; the app must distinguish an observed event from an assumed motive. Human connection, emotional literacy and soft skills are relevant needs; none is interchangeable with a diagnosis of emotional neglect.

## Need statistics

### Recommended for a young-adult audience

CDC's 2022 survey across **26 U.S. states** found **29.7% of adults aged 18–34** received needed social/emotional support only sometimes, rarely or never. Overall adult prevalence was 24.1%; the overall analytic sample was 218,915. Do not generalize this subset to every American. Published June 20, 2024. [CDC MMWR](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/73/wr/mm7324a1.htm).

### National teenage context

CDC/NCHS's nationally representative 2021–2022 survey of U.S. teens aged 12–17 found 58.5% always/usually received needed support. The complement, **about 41.5%**, did not consistently receive it; the weighted analysis used 1,157 teen respondents. This measures perceived support, not neglect or low emotional intelligence. Published July 16, 2024. [CDC/NCHS report](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/nhsr/nhsr206.pdf).

### Childhood emotional neglect

Taillieu and colleagues' 2016 paper used nationally representative **2004–2005** U.S. data from 34,653 adults aged 20+. Emotional neglect only was 6.2%, and emotional neglect with emotional abuse was 3.1%: **approximately 9.3% combined**, calculated from mutually exclusive categories. This is retrospective childhood experience, with the study's measurement threshold; it is not current neglect or the proportion needing an EI app. [Original paper](https://grn.issup.net/files/2018-02/1-s2.0-S0145213416301442-main.pdf).

### Global loneliness context

WHO's June 2025 Commission on Social Connection reporting estimates **15.8%, approximately one in six people globally**, report loneliness. Loneliness concerns a distressing gap between desired and actual connection. It does not imply poor emotional vocabulary, and Bearings has not shown it reduces loneliness. [WHO explanation](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/social-connection).

No defensible population estimate was found for “people who need increased emotional intelligence.” Do not relabel any statistic above to create that number. The often-cited 14.8% childhood emotional-neglect estimate from the original CDC–Kaiser cohort is not a nationally representative percentage of Americans and is not the recommended pitch statistic.

## Emotion education and granularity

**Vedernikova, Kuppens and Erbas, 2021:** a randomized study of 120 adults found that five days of emotion education improved negative emotion differentiation on a scenario-rating measure relative to a control. This supports exploring contextual lessons; the mostly UK/White sample, scenario outcome and inconsistent well-being results limit generalization to daily life or app benefit. [Primary study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.703757/full).

**Matt, Seah and Coifman, 2024:** a brief online emotion-word learning study analyzed 118 college participants and found no significant overall group differences in differentiation, emotional self-efficacy or distress. Exploratory engagement findings are not definitive efficacy evidence. This is a useful counterweight to claiming vocabulary training always works. [Primary study](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0299540).

Design inference: combine short definitions with the writer's own situation, contrast nearby feelings and invite confirmation or rejection. Test whether the writer can apply a distinction in another situation. Neither study validates AI-generated emotional labels or Bearings.

## Yale research

**Yale RULER framework:** Yale's school-wide program teaches recognizing, understanding, labeling, expressing and regulating emotion; nuanced vocabulary is part of labeling. Safe phrasing: “Our design is informed by emotional-education research, including Yale's RULER framework.” Do not imply Yale endorsement, partnership or use of licensed RULER materials. [Official Yale overview](https://medicine.yale.edu/childstudy/services/community-and-schools-programs/center-for-emotional-intelligence/ruler/).

**Brackett and colleagues, 2012:** a 30-week quasi-experimental study of 273 fifth/sixth graders in 15 classrooms across three schools found better year-end grades and teacher-rated social/emotional competence in RULER classrooms. Nonrandom assignment and school/teacher context limit transfer to a self-guided adult app. [Study abstract hosted by Yale](https://isps.yale.edu/resource/enhancing-academic-performance-and-social-and-emotional-competence-with-the-ruler-feeling).

**Rivers and colleagues, 2013:** a cluster randomized trial in 62 schools found improvements in aspects of classroom warmth/connectedness, student autonomy/leadership and attention to students' interests. The intervention was school-wide and teacher-supported; classroom climate is not an individual empathy score. [Primary study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11121-012-0305-2).

## Empathy: a promising hypothesis

**Erbas and colleagues, 2016:** among 98 adults in 49 couples, finer differentiation of one's own negative emotions was associated with better estimates of a partner's emotional valence (r = .30). The association was not the same for positive differentiation or arousal accuracy. This observational study is not Yale-affiliated and does not establish that training increases empathy. [Author-hosted primary paper](https://www.ppw.kuleuven.be/okp/_pdf/Erbas2016FMFYT.pdf).

Safe phrasing: “We want to help people understand their own feelings and practice curiosity about other people's perspectives.” “Bearings increases empathy” remains a hypothesis to evaluate. Suggested future exercise: possible explanations → supporting evidence → a question to ask. Avoid AI declarations about someone else's intentions.

## AI, work and creativity

WEF's 2025 employer survey ranks creative thinking fourth and motivation/self-awareness fifth among core skills; empathy/active listening are also in the top ten. Technical skills remain important. This supports a balanced human-skills narrative, not the claim that coding expertise is obsolete. [WEF skills outlook](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/3-skills-outlook/).

Retain creativity as a possible longer-term direction: noticing curiosity, pride, awe and satisfying experiences could help people explore what matters to them. Do not claim the prototype increases creativity without direct evaluation. Keep the primary demo on emotional literacy and clear communication.

## Human-support resource example

TimelyCare lists TalkNow as 24/7 on-demand mental/emotional support for eligible members of partner institutions. Availability and covered services depend on institutional access. It is a possible link-out, not a Bearings integration or universally free service. [Official support/eligibility information](https://timelycare.com/support/).

The app could help someone compose a minimal request for a call, choose what kind of support they want and contact another person if needed. An automatic broadcast to all friends is not necessary for the core demo; preserve it as a later explicit opt-in idea. Do not promise a response or share journal content automatically.

## AI/ML explanation for the presenter

Current architecture: local saved entries → JournalIntelligence → JournalAnalysisEngine → JournalAnalysisService → separate derived records and corrections. Apple's model generates constrained structured outputs; source segmentation handles limited context, and fingerprints reject stale results after edits. Journal persistence and original writing remain authoritative. Manual emotion choices work when the model is unavailable.

The new feature contract adds finer words, source-linked assumptions and reviewed lesson selection; those are planned until built and tested. They are not a new model trained on private journals. A model is suggesting interpretations, not measuring emotions objectively. Explain how users correct it and how later suggestions use those corrections.

Latest documented limit: real device generation/quality and offline inference have not been accepted because the physical model last reported modelNotReady. Deterministic fixture tests validate app behavior, not real semantic quality. Confirm current device status before recording.

## What to say and avoid

- Say “designed to help,” “research-informed,” “possible pattern” and “the user confirmed.”
- Keep source population/year visible when using a statistic.
- Distinguish empathy, loneliness, emotional neglect and emotional skill development.
- Do not claim treatment, diagnosis, guaranteed calm, proved empathy/creativity gains, Yale endorsement or live generation that did not happen.
- Show one concrete story with a user correction and next-week review. Include an unresolved/partial outcome in testing so the app does not reward only tidy stories.

## Ideas retained beyond the first demo

Positive emotional granularity; a self-selected library of helpful moments; a broader reviewed lesson curriculum; perspective practice; explicitly selected contact groups; and a future study of whether users apply emotional distinctions and communicate needs more clearly. Haptic feedback/UI polish is optional final work. Implementation order is in HacklantaPlan.md.
