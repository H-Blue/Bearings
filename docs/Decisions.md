## Centered timeline and pen shortcut

On October 11, 2026, the owner requested removal of the timeline branding/slogan and bottom helper text, a centered timeline, bottom thumb scrolling, and a small old-school ink-pen shortcut in `#323575`. Center the axis within the safe-area viewport and extend the existing native horizontal scroll surface to the bottom; retain native deceleration and bounded date tap targets. Draw a fountain pen with SwiftUI paths, using the shared opaque navy token and a 44-point accessible button labeled “Write today.” Keep the opening-screen branding, civil dates, month state and journal persistence unchanged.

## Opening screen decision

The owner explicitly selected the right-curving river/ridge map for a minimum two-second opening screen, with Bearings at the top right and the existing slogan underneath. Bundle the accepted image unchanged, use navy native Dynamic Type text within safe areas, and display it for at least two uninterrupted active seconds on a fresh launch. Scene interruptions restart the wait; ordinary foreground returns after completion do not replay it. Initialize storage after the opening and retain its visible loading/error/retry path. The opening uses no animated dismissal and changes no journal persistence behavior.

## Local reflection architecture — October 11, 2026

The owner approved Apple on-device daily analysis, correctable emotions, weekly/monthly reflection and timestamp metadata. The app retains iOS 17 journaling and gates generation by SDK, iOS 26+, device, Apple Intelligence/model readiness and locale. Original writing remains separate from interpretations. Corrections have independent persistent records and survive regeneration. Safe optional schema additions preserve unknown historical creation zones; file-backed old-schema migration is tested.

Weeks run Monday through Sunday in Gregorian civil space. Monthly inputs use clipped weeks plus independent daily facts, preventing out-of-month experiences and reducing factual loss through repeated summaries. All generation uses fresh on-device sessions, bounded source segments and serial inference; stale results fail a committed-source fingerprint check before saving. Emotion rings are distinct from owner-mandated navy linework and grey writing markers; their accessible labels name the emotion. New native reflection/metadata controls are minimal adaptations without newly approved Figma screens. Detailed decisions and privacy/backup limits are in LocalIntelligence.md.
