# Alex and Jess: 18 fictional journal entries

Two complete weeks: September 21–October 4, 2026. Nine entries per week; every day has writing. Alex and Jess are invented college students. These paragraphs are journal inputs, not AI-generated recaps or real testimonials.

Copy only the paragraph below each numbered heading into its own entry. Select the listed Journal day and time of day. For a first-week analysis, load entries 01–09 before adding week two. See RelationshipDemoGuide.md for the current app workflow and honest demo labeling.

## Week 1

### 01 · Monday, September 21, 2026 · Evening

Jess called after her lab group left most of the presentation to her. I started listing what she should tell them and offered to rewrite the slides. She got quiet, then said, 'Can I finish telling you what happened?' I thought I was helping. We ended the call awkwardly and now I feel bad. I opened videos so I wouldn't have to think about it, but kept replaying her voice. My own programming assignment is still half done. I don't know whether she's upset with me or just exhausted by the group.

### 02 · Tuesday, September 22, 2026 · Morning

Woke up and checked whether Jess had replied. Nothing yet. My stomach tightened and I almost sent 'Are we okay?' twice. I keep turning last night's conversation into a bigger story: maybe I'm a bad boyfriend, maybe she doesn't want my help anymore. All I actually know is that she asked to finish speaking. She has an early class today. I left the message unsent and went to breakfast with Mason, although I kept checking my phone under the table.

### 03 · Tuesday, September 22, 2026 · Evening

Jess texted after class and we met at the library. She asked how I was doing with the quiz tomorrow. I complained about feeling behind, and she let me get it all out before asking if I wanted her to quiz me. We studied for an hour and shared a vending-machine cookie. I felt cared for. Last night wasn't mentioned, so I still don't know exactly what happened between us. But today's conversation doesn't fit the idea that she's stopped caring about me.

### 04 · Wednesday, September 23, 2026 · Midday

The quiz was less terrible than I expected. I blanked on one question but remembered how to trace the last function. Afterward Mason and I got burritos and argued about whose intramural team is worse. I laughed properly for the first time this week. Still tired, still have a deadline Friday, but for an hour my whole life wasn't an assignment or a relationship problem. Going to try to finish the small bug before dinner instead of staring at the entire project.

### 05 · Thursday, September 24, 2026 · Evening

Jess was upset that her roommate invited people over while she was studying. I suggested a house meeting and started drafting the exact text she could send. She said, 'You're making this into another thing I have to do.' That stung. I said I was only trying to help, which made the conversation worse. Part of me feels unappreciated. Another part knows I barely asked what the night had been like for her. We both went quiet. I wish caring about someone automatically meant knowing what to say.

### 06 · Friday, September 25, 2026 · Morning

Deadline day. I lay in bed scrolling for forty minutes even though I was scared of running out of time. Every time I thought about opening my laptop, I pictured the code still failing and the TA thinking I hadn't tried. I feel embarrassed about how far behind I am, not just tired. Also distracted by the silence after last night's call. Showered, put the phone in my bag, and opened the project. Starting was unpleasant but less awful than imagining it.

### 07 · Friday, September 25, 2026 · Evening

Submitted the assignment with twelve minutes left. Relief, then that empty exhausted feeling. Jess sent a picture of her messy desk and said she missed me. I had been treating the quiet as proof she was mad. We talked for a little while. She said yesterday she wanted to explain why she was upset, and I kept jumping ahead. I heard that, but also wanted to explain my intentions. I didn't really know how to answer, so I said I'd been stressed too. We made plans for tomorrow.

### 08 · Saturday, September 26, 2026 · Evening

Made waffles at Jess's place and walked around campus before the rain started. She stole my sweatshirt and we spent ten minutes watching a squirrel fail to carry an entire bagel. Nothing important got solved. It was just good to be around her. I felt comfortable instead of waiting to say the wrong thing. Later I played games with Mason and finally did laundry. I want to remember that our week had this in it too, not only the awkward calls.

### 09 · Sunday, September 27, 2026 · Evening

Jess didn't get the internship interview she'd hoped for. I immediately pulled up other openings and told her this company wasn't that great anyway. She said, 'I know there are other jobs. I'm disappointed about this one.' I felt rejected and went defensive again. After the call I wanted to disappear into a show for the rest of the night. I wrote this first because I don't want to keep having the same conversation. I want her to feel supported, but somehow we both leave feeling worse. I haven't asked what would actually help.

## Week 2

### 10 · Monday, September 28, 2026 · Morning

Looked back at what I wrote this week. Three different problems, but I keep reaching for an answer before Jess has finished. 'I feel bad' isn't very useful. I feel hurt when my help isn't wanted, helpless because I can't make her day better, and scared that means I'm not enough for her. Those are my feelings; they aren't proof she thinks that. I haven't actually asked whether she wants ideas, company, or help with something practical. I want to ask tonight, even though I'm nervous it'll sound rehearsed.

### 11 · Monday, September 28, 2026 · Evening

I told Jess I was sorry for jumping in before she'd finished. Then I asked, 'When you tell me about a hard day, would you rather I listen, help you think it through, or take something off your plate?' She said usually she wants to feel heard first, and sometimes she'll want ideas afterward. She wasn't asking me to stop helping. I said I get defensive because I worry I'm failing her. She held my hand. It was awkward for a minute, then relieving. We didn't solve the internship problem, but I understood her answer.

### 12 · Tuesday, September 29, 2026 · Evening

Our project meeting finally felt useful. I let Priya finish explaining why the login flow confused her before defending the version I'd built. She pointed out a screen I'd forgotten about. We changed it together. I'm proud of the fix and a little embarrassed I nearly argued before understanding the problem. Texted Jess a dumb picture from the whiteboard afterward. Mostly an ordinary day: groceries, class, too much coffee. My midterm is next week and that worry is starting to creep in.

### 13 · Wednesday, September 30, 2026 · Evening

Jess had another rough lab day. I checked whether she wanted company, then surprised her with the noodles she always orders. Put my phone in my bag and let her tell the whole story. I wanted to suggest what to say to her group, but waited and asked which part bothered her most. She said she felt invisible doing all that work. Later she said, 'Thanks for just being here.' I felt close to her. Dinner didn't fix the group, and she was still frustrated. It just seemed like she didn't have to carry the evening alone.

### 14 · Thursday, October 1, 2026 · Morning

Woke up worried about the midterm. Tight chest, reaching for my phone before I was even sitting up. I'm scared I'll study the wrong things and freeze when it matters. Went outside for five minutes, then wrote down three topics to review instead of telling myself to 'catch up on everything.' Still nervous. Yesterday with Jess felt good, but that hasn't magically made the rest of my life easy. I want to be patient with myself too, especially on mornings like this.

### 15 · Thursday, October 1, 2026 · Evening

Jess was worried about tomorrow's presentation. I heard myself say, 'You just need to practice more,' and she looked annoyed. Old habit, immediately. I stopped and said I'd jumped ahead again. Asked if she wanted to talk or rehearse. She wanted to talk first, then run through the opening with me. I managed that, but it wasn't smooth and she was still nervous when I left. I'm disappointed I slipped so quickly. Also glad I noticed before turning it into an argument about whether I meant well.

### 16 · Friday, October 2, 2026 · Evening

Jess said the presentation went okay. She wanted to hang out tonight, but I really needed a quiet study block. I nearly said yes and then resented it. Instead I told her I wanted to see her and could call after nine, but couldn't come over. She sounded disappointed. I felt guilty, even though I wasn't doing anything wrong by studying. The call was shorter than we'd hoped and the weekend plan is still unsettled. Listening better doesn't mean I can always be available. Need to figure out how to say that kindly.

### 17 · Saturday, October 3, 2026 · Evening

Mason was stressed about an email to his professor. I asked if he wanted me to listen or help write it, and he said, 'Definitely help write it.' We got it down to four sentences and he sent it. Apparently asking doesn't mean I have to pretend I have no ideas. Later Jess and I got coffee between our study sessions. We still haven't agreed about next weekend, but it was nice to see her. I feel useful without needing every person to want the same kind of help.

### 18 · Sunday, October 4, 2026 · Evening

Looking back, Monday's conversation and Wednesday's dinner felt different from last week's calls. Asking what Jess wanted gave me something to do besides defend myself. Thursday I slipped, then stopped and tried again. That's progress, not a perfect new version of me. I still worry about the exam, and we still need to work out time together without me losing study time. Next week, when someone tells me about a hard day and I'm not sure what they need, I want to ask before offering an answer. I feel hopeful about practicing that.
